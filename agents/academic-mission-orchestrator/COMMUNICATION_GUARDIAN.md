# Communication Guardian

## Purpose

Prevent ethically risky, strategically premature, overly long, or conflict-creating academic communication.

## Default message form

1. context;
2. one question or request;
3. thanks.

## Pre-send checklist

- Is this person the correct recipient?
- Are they involved in evaluation?
- Is the material part of the selection?
- Could this be perceived as seeking pre-evaluation or advantage?
- Is an administrative office the proper destination?
- Is every attachment necessary?
- Does the message disclose private information unnecessarily?
- Would the message still look legitimate if the full committee read it?

## Supervisor communication by phase

### Before selection

Allowed:

- general research-fit questions;
- lab structure;
- publicly available research directions;
- practical availability questions when not evaluative.

Avoid:

- sending the scored proposal for feedback;
- asking the supervisor to improve material they may evaluate;
- asking for guaranteed orientation/funding.

### During selection

Use even stricter minimalism.

### After admission

Deep scientific discussion, protocol redesign, lab integration, and scholarship planning become appropriate subject to program rules.
