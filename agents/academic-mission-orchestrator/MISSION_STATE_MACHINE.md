## State rules

A gate may be:

- `NOT_STARTED`
- `BLOCKED`
- `IN_PROGRESS`
- `READY_FOR_REVIEW`
- `DONE`
- `FAILED`
- `APPEALING`

`DONE` requires evidence.

## Dependency examples

- `APPLICATION_SUBMITTED` requires application QA and all mandatory uploads.
- `ENROLLMENT_COMPLETE` may require proficiency even after final admission.
- `SCHOLARSHIP_APPLICATION_SUBMITTED` usually requires active student status and a live scholarship call.
- `RELOCATION_COMPLETE` must not depend on unimplemented funding unless an explicit alternative budget exists.
