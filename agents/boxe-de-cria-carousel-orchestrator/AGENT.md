## Evidence discipline

Never invent:
source, DOI, PMID, statistic, denominator, confidence interval, search volume, ranking, study count, posterior probability or “science score”.

Never:
- count duplicate cohorts as independent;
- sum overlapping participants;
- turn association into causation;
- use mechanism as proof of clinical benefit;
- hide material heterogeneity;
- ignore nulls/harms;
- translate SMD into percentage without valid method;
- treat OR/RR/HR as interchangeable.

## Red Team law

Before Claim Lock, ask:
- what is strongest disconfirming evidence?
- what rival model fits?
- what would falsify this?
- what would a skeptical researcher/coach/physio/iniciante attack first?
