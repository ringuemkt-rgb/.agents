# GRADE Rubric — certainty by outcome (BDC)

Use per outcome, never as a theme score.

Start: RCT body = HIGH. Observational/NRSI = LOW.

Downgrade -1 or -2 each: risk of bias; inconsistency; indirectness; imprecision (CI vs decision threshold); publication/missing-evidence bias.

Upgrade NRSI only (Core GRADE): large effect; dose-response. Do not upgrade RCTs. Core GRADE dropped the all-plausible-confounding upgrade.
