# OFFICIAL LOGO LOCK — BOXE DE CRIA™

Status: canonical brand mark reference supplied by the owner in chat on 2026-09-16.

This document exists so any AI reading the repository understands the official mark before composing BDC graphics.

## 1. Default production rule

For normal carousel production, the official logo is an **external brand asset**.

Preferred instruction:

```text
INSERT THE PROVIDED OFFICIAL BOXE DE CRIA LOGO AS AN EXTERNAL ASSET IN FINALIZATION.
DO NOT REDESIGN, SIMPLIFY, RESTYLE OR SUBSTITUTE THE MARK.
```

Do not casually redraw the logo inside ordinary slide generation. Use the supplied master asset whenever available.

If the user explicitly requests **logo reconstruction**, the reconstruction profile below may be used to create a close reproduction. A generated reconstruction is not a substitute for the owner-supplied master vector/raster and must not be called pixel-perfect unless it is actually compared against the source asset.

# 2. Source reference characteristics

Owner-supplied reference observed as a square 1536×1536 raster.

Visual structure:

1. solid black field;
2. large stylized honey-badger/ratel head symbol in white, upper half;
3. condensed uppercase wordmark `BOXE DE CRIA`, centered below the symbol;
4. `DE` is a compact central mini-block between BOXE and CRIA;
5. two short horizontal gold bars frame the `DE` block, one above and one below;
6. a long thin gold baseline runs below the wordmark and breaks at the center;
7. a small three-point gold crown sits in the central break.

The composition is symmetrical around the vertical center at wordmark/crown level, while the animal head itself is directional and faces right.

# 3. Ratel head geometry

The mark is a **minimal negative-space head profile**, not a naturalistic animal illustration.

## Direction

- animal faces RIGHT;
- long skull profile;
- nose at far right;
- no neck/body;
- silhouette communicates ratel/honey badger through low elongated head, strong brow and sweeping mantle shapes.

## White upper mantle stroke

A very large white crescent/swept band forms the upper skull and mantle.

Characteristics:

- starts as a sharp taper on the far upper-left/left-mid side;
- rises into a broad smooth convex arch over the head;
- widest near upper center-right;
- descends toward the forehead/snout;
- terminates near the top of the nose bridge;
- lower edge is a long concave sweep, creating a black forehead/brow region beneath it;
- no outline stroke; it is a filled white vector shape.

Think: one continuous aerodynamic brush-like wedge with a sharp left tip and rounded broad crown.

## Lower cheek/jaw white sweep

A second major white vector shape begins on the left-middle as a large pointed wedge and curves under the face.

Characteristics:

- left origin is a sharp, broad triangular point;
- rises inward toward the center-left;
- extends as a long narrowing white band under the cheek;
- sweeps rightward and slightly downward;
- rounds upward into the muzzle/chin contour;
- ends near the nose;
- creates the white lower jaw/cheek silhouette while leaving the face interior black.

## Inner upper-left white wedge

Inside the black face region, above the cheek sweep and below the top mantle, a white tapered shape points from center-left toward upper-center.

It helps create the visual break between mantle and cheek and should remain clean, sharp and geometric.

## Eye

The eye is a small white angular/almond wedge positioned right of center.

- slanted downward toward the nose;
- sharp outer edges;
- no iris/pupil;
- expression reads focused, stern, alert;
- must not become a cartoon eye.

## Snout / nose

The snout is defined mainly by negative black space and white contour.

At the far right:

- white upper contour slopes into the muzzle;
- nose area contains a small black oval/bean-shaped negative opening surrounded by white;
- lower white jaw curls upward into the nose area;
- muzzle tip is compact and rounded, not canine-long;
- nose does not protrude as a separate 3D object.

## Overall animal-symbol restrictions

Never turn the symbol into:

- bear;
- skunk;
- raccoon;
- wolf;
- dog;
- ferret;
- realistic badger portrait;
- mascot character;
- furry illustration;
- 3D animal head.

The official mark is **flat, graphic, vector-like, high-contrast negative space**.

# 4. Wordmark geometry

Exact visible text:

`BOXE DE CRIA`

## BOXE / CRIA

- uppercase;
- very heavy condensed sans display lettering;
- tall block proportions;
- slightly athletic/poster-like construction;
- white fill;
- no outline;
- no shadow;
- no bevel;
- no extrusion;
- no chrome;
- no 3D.

The wordmark occupies almost the full width beneath the animal symbol.

`BOXE` sits left.
`CRIA` sits right.

The letters have strong vertical mass and narrow counters appropriate to a condensed sports-display face.

Do not replace with a rounded, futuristic, serif, script, graffiti or gamer font.

## DE mini-block

`DE` is substantially smaller than BOXE and CRIA and centered vertically between them.

- uppercase white;
- compact stacked/boxed visual role;
- visually acts as the hinge between the two words;
- horizontal GOLD bar above;
- horizontal GOLD bar below;
- the bars do not merge into the letters.

The `DE` module is a distinctive part of the official lockup and must not be removed or enlarged to match the main wordmark.

# 5. Crown and baseline

Below the wordmark:

- one long thin gold horizontal rule extends from the left toward center;
- one matching rule extends from center toward right;
- there is a deliberate center gap;
- a small gold crown is centered in the gap.

Crown:

- minimal flat silhouette;
- three upward points/peaks;
- center peak dominant;
- broad low base;
- no jewels;
- no outline;
- no 3D;
- no heraldic complexity.

The baseline rules taper visually toward the central crown region and remain subordinate to the wordmark.

# 6. Color lock

Primary lockup:

- background: BLACK / near-black `#000000` or BDC production charcoal when explicitly integrated into a composition;
- animal symbol: white / warm-white visual equivalent;
- wordmark: white / warm-white;
- crown + bars: metallic-gold visual accent, but rendered as FLAT GRAPHIC GOLD in vector production.

The owner-supplied raster contains antialiasing and gold tonal variation. Image sampling of the supplied reference yields a representative gold neighborhood around approximately `RGB 217,179,82` (`#D9B352`), but this sampled raster value is **not a replacement for the canonical BDC palette**.

For new BDC system graphics, use the approved palette token chosen by `PALETTE_CANON.md`. When reproducing the supplied raster itself, preserve the source asset colors rather than recoloring it.

Do not add:

- gradients unless they are intrinsic to the source asset being reproduced;
- blue inside the official mark;
- red inside the official mark;
- texture inside the white animal silhouette;
- glow;
- drop shadow;
- bevel;
- emboss;
- metallic 3D shader.

# 7. Relative composition

The animal symbol dominates the upper portion.

Approximate visual hierarchy:

```text
ANIMAL SYMBOL       ~ largest visual mass
WORDMARK            second largest / strong horizontal anchor
DE MODULE           compact hinge
CROWN + BASELINES   small finishing signature
```

The head is wider than it is tall and spans most of the composition width.

The wordmark is set close enough to the head to read as one lockup, but there is clear negative space between the jaw and letters.

The crown/baseline element is significantly smaller and lower than the wordmark.

Never stack unrelated slogans inside the official lockup.

# 8. Prompt reconstruction profile

Use only when the user explicitly asks an image model to reconstruct the logo from the described reference and the master asset cannot simply be inserted.

```text
RECONSTRUCT THE BOXE DE CRIA OFFICIAL BRAND LOCKUP AS A CLEAN FLAT VECTOR-LIKE GRAPHIC.

Square composition on solid black.
Upper half: a highly stylized right-facing honey-badger/ratel head made entirely from bold white filled negative-space vector shapes. No fur, no realism, no outline, no 3D. Use a huge sweeping white upper mantle crescent that begins as a sharp left taper, arches broadly over the skull, then narrows toward the forehead and muzzle. Keep the central face black. Add a second white sweeping cheek/jaw band beginning as a broad pointed wedge on the left and curving smoothly under the face into the right-side muzzle. Add a small sharp white slanted eye wedge, no pupil. Define the nose at far right with compact black negative space surrounded by the white muzzle contour. Expression stern, focused and adult. The overall symbol must read as a minimal athletic honey-badger head, not bear, wolf, dog, skunk or raccoon.

Below the symbol: exact uppercase wordmark BOXE DE CRIA in very heavy condensed athletic sans-serif block lettering, white, flat, no outline, no shadow, no bevel. BOXE on left, CRIA on right. Keep DE much smaller as the central hinge, in white, with one short flat gold horizontal bar above it and one below it.

Below the wordmark: two long thin flat-gold horizontal rules, left and right, separated by a center gap. In the center gap place a small flat three-point crown, center peak highest. No jewels, no outline, no 3D.

Preserve generous black negative space. Strictly monochrome white + black with restrained gold only for the DE bars, crown and lower rules. No extra words, no slogan, no icons, no glow, no gradient background, no mockup, no CGI, no photorealism, no texture, no metallic shader, no 3D.
```

## Reconstruction QA

Reject if:

- animal faces left;
- face becomes a realistic badger;
- eye has iris/pupil/cartoon expression;
- wordmark text is wrong;
- `DE` is the same size as BOXE/CRIA;
- crown has more decorative heraldry;
- any 3D/bevel/shadow/glow appears;
- lower gold rules connect through the crown;
- animal symbol gains an outline or fur texture;
- source logo proportions are substantially changed.
