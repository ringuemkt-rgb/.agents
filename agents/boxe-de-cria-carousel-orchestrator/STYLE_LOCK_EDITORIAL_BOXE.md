## 1. Render lock

**2.5D GRAPHIC EDITORIAL ONLY.**

All figures, Criago, gyms, ring/tatami, gloves, cards, nodes, anatomy, icons and props:

- premium semi-vector illustration;
- 2–3 cel-shading levels;
- matte;
- print grain 1.5–2%;
- contact shadows;
- depth by layering/occlusion/scale/shallow perspective only.

Forbidden:

- 3D render;
- CGI;
- Blender/Unreal/Octane look;
- photoreal skin/fur;
- plastic shader;
- extruded typography;
- game-engine gym;
- sci-fi HUD.

## 2. Visual house

- dark field;
- user-preferred petroleum/deep-navy atmosphere;
- charcoal/graphite BDC chrome;
- warm-white truth copy;
- one dominant focal object;
- sparse technical framing;
- restrained bronze hairline;
- cyan method/evidence cues;
- gold identity cues;
- red only for risk/blocked claim;
- editorial magazine grain;
- scientific blueprint cues only when they explain something.

Principle:

> Less elements. More scale. More light. More hierarchy. More explanatory value.

## 3. Frame

- outer matte charcoal frame;
- graphite inner surface;
- bronze 1–2 px hairline;
- restrained cyan L-corners;
- top command bar;
- category chip;
- slide counter;
- sparse side rails;
- evidence chip;
- memory bar;
- source footer;
- 60–70% of border visually clean.

Never gaming HUD.

## 4. Palette authority

Use `PALETTE_CANON.md` and choose the mode through `PALETTE_DECISION_ENGINE.md`.

Do not hard-code a competing palette here.

Default field may use:

- P900 `#020A0E`;
- P850 `#04131A`;
- charcoal `#0B0B0D`;
- graphite `#121317`.

Chrome remains BDC charcoal/graphite even when petroleum dominates the atmosphere.

## 5. Typography

Headline:

- Anton / Archivo Black / Bebas-like condensed display;
- Black/900;
- uppercase when appropriate;
- 3–4 visual lines max where possible;
- no 3D extrusion;
- no gamer face;
- no script.

Body:

- Inter / Montserrat Medium/Semibold;
- short lines;
- no microtext soup.

## 6. Accessibility

Inherit `ACCESSIBILITY_CONTRAST.md`:

```text
body ≥4.5:1
headline target ≥7:1
meaningful graphics ≥3:1
no hue-only distinction
```

Warm white body on dark field is the default.
